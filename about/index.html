About Vanessa Awo

I am an AWS Certified Solutions Architect building hands-on cloud architecture experience and preparing for Solutions Architect, Solutions Engineer, and Cloud Consultant roles. My goal is to bring together technical cloud knowledge, strong communication skills, and more than three years of client-facing professional experience to help organizations design practical, secure, and scalable cloud solutions.

My hands-on AWS experience spans core infrastructure: VPC, EC2, IAM, S3, RDS, Lambda, CloudFront, Route 53, Auto Scaling, and CloudWatch. Through architecture projects, I have designed high-availability solutions using Multi-AZ deployments, applied the AWS Well-Architected Framework across all six pillars, and practiced foundational infrastructure provisioning using Terraform and CloudFormation.

I completed a B.S. in Computer Science at Western Governors University in June 2026 and am completing the AWS re/Start program through Per Scholas in Seattle, expected August 2026. My certifications include the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) and LPI Linux Essentials.

Before moving into cloud, I spent more than three years managing over 100 concurrent client cases as a Claims Adjuster and Customer Solutions Associate. Those roles taught me how to gather requirements from multiple stakeholders, communicate complex information clearly under deadline pressure, own case resolution end-to-end, and work across teams to drive outcomes. These are skills that map directly onto Solutions Architecture and Solutions Engineering work.

I am seeking entry-level opportunities where I can contribute immediately, continue learning, and grow within a cloud-focused team.
